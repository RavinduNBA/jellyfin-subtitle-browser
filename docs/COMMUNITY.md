# Sharing and community

Start with a small group of testers who want subtitle history or use subtitles for language learning. Link the install guide, compatibility table, source and short demo. Label it an unsigned early alpha and disclose the primarily AI-developed implementation.

Do not promise compatibility or stability beyond the tests actually performed. Route this fork's issues to its own tracker. Preserve upstream credits and license notices.

If sharing in official Jellyfin spaces, read the current rules first: https://jellyfin.org/docs/general/contributing/llm-policies/ . Their policy prohibits AI-generated community posts and requires disclosure for primarily AI-developed third-party projects. Write any public announcement yourself from your experience; this repository does not provide a ready-to-post AI-written announcement.

No community posts, emails, invitations or messages are sent automatically by this project.
